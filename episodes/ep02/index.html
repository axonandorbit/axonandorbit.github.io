Your brain isn't one organ. It's two.

Watch on YouTube

Stanford researchers traced where the brain comes from and found two separate progenitor populations: Otx2+ cells build the forebrain and midbrain, Gbx2+ cells build the hindbrain, and they don't overlap from the earliest stages (mouse embryos). The same two-origin pattern shows up in chickens, zebrafish and acorn worms, pointing back over 500 million years. The team also grew functional human hindbrain motor neurons from stem cells for the first time, a new model for studying diseases like ALS and SMA.
