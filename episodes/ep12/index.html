AI language models have a hidden confidence dial that decides when they answer

Watch on YouTube

Do language models actually use their own confidence when deciding whether to answer or abstain? Researchers from Google DeepMind and Princeton built a four-phase test. Models applied an implicit threshold to internal confidence when abstaining. With activation steering, boosting confidence decreased abstention and suppressing it increased abstention, causal evidence that confidence drives the behaviour. Verbal (stated) confidence predicted abstention too, but both it and token probabilities were lossy read-outs of a richer internal representation.
