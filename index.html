How we work

  • Primary sources only. Each episode cites the journal, date and DOI on screen and below.

  • Original visuals. All animation is made by us in Blender. It is illustrative, not imaging data.
  • Synthetic narration. The voice is a stock AI voice, not a clone of a real person.
