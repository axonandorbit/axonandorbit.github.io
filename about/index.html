About & method

Axon & Orbit turns brand-new research into short, cinematic explainers, so that the most surprising findings in science reach people the week they are published, not years later.

How an episode is made

  1. Find. Every day we scan new papers in Nature, Science, Cell, PNAS, Neuron, Science Robotics, ApJ, PRL and other journals, plus major security advisories and graphics research (SIGGRAPH).
  2. Verify. Numbers come only from the paper or the official press release, read at the source. Preprints are labelled as such.
  3. Write. One idea per episode, the key number, and the caveat that limits it.
  4. Build. Every visual is original 3D, made in Blender in our own brand style. It illustrates the idea; it is not real imaging data.
  5. Voice. Narration uses a synthetic stock voice (not a clone of any real person) so episodes can follow the news quickly.
  6. Check. Automatic checks for legibility and flashing images, then a final human review before release.

What we don't do

  • No sponsored science, no paid placements.
  • No exploitation details in security episodes: we describe the flaw and tell you to update.
  • No realistic depictions of real people.
