Corrections policy

Science moves fast and we can get things wrong. If you find an error in an episode (a number, a claim, a missing caveat), email leoronchi4@gmail.com with the episode title and, if possible, the source.

  • We check every report against the original paper.
  • Confirmed errors are corrected in the video description, in a pinned comment and on the episode page here, with the date of the correction.
  • If an error changes the meaning of an episode, we add an on-screen correction or take the video down and say why.
